02 · Strategy

Why the website was redesigned

Move from a dense brochure to a booking-first story — calm, reliable, open-road — without losing the routes, rates, and coverage people need.

Problem

Old layout packed stats, maps, and lists into the first screens. Felt like a dashboard, not a driver you trust.

Visual fix

Full-bleed hero, brand as the signal, collage intro, square CTAs, soft fades only. Leaf wash / canopy black / foliage green / brass.

Product fix

Keep every useful section — van, routes, services, pricing, coverage, places — ordered as a story that ends in WhatsApp booking.

For visitors

  • · One clear job per section
  • · Flat rates visible before the form
  • · Path: see van → pick service → book

For the operator

  • · Brand mark stays consistent everywhere
  • · WhatsApp handoff with structured details
  • · Coverage + places prove local knowledge

03 · Verbal mark

Why “Airport to coast across Iloilo.”

So people remember the offer in four words: private van, your timing — not a shared ride, not a tour package.

Whole vehicle for the group. You set pickup, stops, and pace. Licensed driver included.

Where it lives

Hero support line, invite band, WhatsApp opener, booking confirmation tone.

Desired result

Hear or read the line → think Xplore Odyssey Iloilo. Cheap to repeat, hard to confuse with Grab.

Hear the line → remember the van.

09 · Photography

Place first. Van second.

Hero and intro lead with Iloilo — esplanade, churches, islands. The Hiace shows up later as proof of how you ride, not what you’re buying.

Vehicle proof

Use after routes and services. Soft gradient for type. Never the first-viewport story.

Capacity

Side and cabin shots for “what you ride in.” Attraction photos carry destination and day-hire inspiration.

10 · Voice

Straight talk. Local roads.

Use

  • Short, direct sentences
  • Local place names (Ajuy, Estancia, Panay)
  • Concrete promises: on time, whole van, WhatsApp reply
  • CTAs: “Book a van”, “See pricing”, “Send request”

Avoid

  • Generic travel brochure fluff
  • “Revolutionize your journey” AI copy
  • Emoji clutter in primary UI
  • Surge / gimmick pricing language

11 · UI

Components

Buttons

Primary = ink fill. Secondary = outline. Prices = brass. Dark bands use mist fill for Book. Radius stays tight (near-square) — poster energy, not soft fintech pills.

Book a vanSee pricing

Patterns

  • · Soft fades only — no bounce or glow
  • · One job per section; hero stays sparse
  • · Pricing always linked from hero
  • · Book always in the header
  • · Forms open WhatsApp with structured text

12 · Guardrails

Keep the road clear.

Do

  • · Keep XO / wordmark intact — no stretch
  • · Leaf wash + canopy black as the base pair
  • · Real van and Iloilo photography
  • · Flat PHP rates with includes listed
  • · Book CTA visible in header and invite

Don’t

  • · Recolor brand into purple / neon / orange
  • · Pack the hero with chips, stats, overlays
  • · Rounded-full pill UI everywhere
  • · Stock tropical collage that could be any island
  • · Let a headline overpower Xplore Odyssey
