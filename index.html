A few places worth the drive

The Iloilo worth seeing.

Hire the van for the day and string a few of these together. We’ll handle the route, the timing, and the long stretches in between.

No. 01 · Miag-ao

Miag-ao Church

A UNESCO World Heritage fortress-church carved from yellow sandstone in 1797.

No. 02 · Carles

Gigantes Islands

A scatter of limestone islets, hidden lagoons, and the country's sweetest scallops.

No. 03 · Jaro, Iloilo City

Jaro Cathedral

The grand belfry and shrine of Our Lady of the Candles, heart of old Iloilo.

No. 04 · San Joaquin

San Joaquin Church

A coral-stone church with a militant Spanish-era facade facing the sea.

No. 05 · Molo, Iloilo City

Molo Church

Gothic-Renaissance spires and an all-female roster of saints inside.

No. 06 · Iloilo City

Iloilo River Esplanade

A long riverside walk, best in the late afternoon when the city slows down.

No. 07 · Leon

Bucari Pine Forest

Cool pine ridges and grasslands locals call “the little Baguio of Iloilo.”

No. 08 · Carles

Sicogon Island

White sand, palm groves, and quiet coves a short banca ride from the mainland.
